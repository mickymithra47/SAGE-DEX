# 26 — Block Finality & Data Confidence Tiers

## 1. Finality Tiers
- **Observed (0-1 Confirmations)**: Instant ingestion, flagged as unfinalized; subject to reorg.
- **Confirmed (2-63 Confirmations)**: Stable; low reorg probability.
- **Finalized (>= 64 Confirmations / PoS Finality)**: Mathematically immutable on Ethereum L1.
